About

I build machine-learning methods for prognostics and health management (PHM). At the Centre de Mathématiques Appliquées (CMAP) at École Polytechnique, I design fault-detection, diagnostics, and predictive-maintenance models, integrating multi-sensor datasets with a focus on battery health management.

Previously, at CEA LIST, I developed decentralised and robust federated-learning algorithms for secure distributed AI, and engineered countermeasures against backdoor and poisoning attacks to strengthen the trustworthiness of AI in industrial settings. Earlier work at MedUni Graz and AIT covered interpretable and explainable ML for healthcare and human activity recognition for ambient assisted living.

I hold a PhD in Computer Science from TU Graz and was a Marie Skłodowska–Curie fellow in the H2020 ACROSSING network.

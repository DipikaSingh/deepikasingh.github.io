Deepika Singh

Research Engineer at CMAP, École Polytechnique

CMAP, École Polytechnique

Palaiseau, France

I am a Research Engineer at the Centre de Mathématiques Appliquées (CMAP), École Polytechnique, where I build machine-learning methods for prognostics and health management (PHM) — fault detection, diagnostics, and predictive maintenance — from multi-sensor operational data, with a current focus on battery health management.

Before joining CMAP in July 2024, I was a postdoctoral researcher at CEA LIST (Paris-Saclay), developing decentralised and robust federated-learning algorithms for secure distributed AI and designing countermeasures against backdoor and poisoning attacks. Earlier, at the Medical University of Graz I contributed to the EU FeatureCloud project on privacy-preserving healthcare AI, and at the Austrian Institute of Technology (AIT) I worked on interpretable and explainable ML and human activity recognition for ambient assisted living.

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at Graz University of Technology in 2021, advised by Andreas Holzinger, as a Marie Skłodowska–Curie fellow in the H2020 ACROSSING Innovative Training Network.

Research interests: prognostics & health management, battery degradation modelling, federated & trustworthy AI, explainable AI, human activity recognition, predictive maintenance.
